About Me

Information Security Engineer · Innovative Solutions SA · Riyadh, KSA

I am an accomplished PAM consultant with over 6 years of hands-on experience in Privileged Access Management. My work spans the full lifecycle — strategy, HLD/LLD architecture, deployment, integration, DR drills, automation and operations — for large-scale, security-critical environments.
